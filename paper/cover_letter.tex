\begin{letter}{The Editor\\The Journal of Supercomputing}
\opening{Dear Editor,}

Please consider the enclosed manuscript ``Neural Pre-Decoding for the Surface Code on Four FPGA Nodes: Integer
Inference of Convolutional Pre-Decoders on Alveo U55C Accelerators'' for publication in The Journal of Supercomputing.

Neural pre-decoders are a recent proposal for fast decoding of quantum error correcting codes: a convolutional network
removes local errors from the syndrome before a matching decoder processes the remainder. The manuscript implements
the two pre-decoders that NVIDIA released with pretrained weights as one integer HLS kernel on four Alveo U55C cards,
each card an independent node. The kernel is bit exact with an integer model of the networks on every instance of
every card, and the integer model is judged with NVIDIA's unmodified evaluation code, with which we also reproduce the
published accuracy of both networks. Four cards process \NumFourFast{} and \NumFourAcc{} windows per second of the
two networks with linear scaling and within two percent of the throughput predicted by the kernel schedule. The
manuscript reports openly that against a tuned CPU baseline the advantage of the cards is modest
(\NumSpeedFast{} and \NumSpeedAcc{} times), and quantifies, from the arithmetic per window, how far these networks are
from real time decoding at microsecond syndrome cycles. Every reported number is generated by script from raw measurement records.

A related manuscript by the author, a distributed statevector simulator on the same four cards, is under revision at
The Journal of Supercomputing (Research Square preprint 10.21203/rs.3.rs-10128153/v1); the present work adopts its
treatment of the cards as nodes but shares no results with it.

The manuscript is not under consideration elsewhere. Every reference was checked against Crossref or arXiv, apart from
the vendor documents and model pages, which were checked at their sources. Thank you for your consideration.

\closing{Sincerely,}
